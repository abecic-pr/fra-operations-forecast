# FRA Operations Forecast — identity exploration

Status: Flightpath (A) selected by the user on 30 October 2026.

## Brief

Create a clean, quiet identity for a Frankfurt Airport forecasting prototype.
The user asked for an Apple-like visual character: clarity, generous space,
carefully balanced geometry and restrained colour. Keep the existing project
name. The audience is a person monitoring runway direction and weather
suitability, plus the hackathon judges. The immediate uses are the app header,
browser favicon and demo presentation.

Brand qualities: calm, precise, legible, considered.

The design should make data easier to interpret. Forecasts, observed runway use,
and official runway status must remain visibly separate. Baseline rule outputs
must not acquire invented confidence percentages through visual styling.

Avoid: aircraft silhouettes, clouds with arrows, radar sweeps, compass roses,
shields, gradients in the logo, and imitation Apple or airport partner marks.

## Intended visual direction

- Soft white canvas, white surfaces, graphite type and one blue accent.
- System sans-serif in the app; medium weight headings and tabular data figures.
- Spacious hierarchy and fine separators, with restrained corner radii.
- Labels and shapes accompany every semantic colour.
- Solid lines for observations; dashed lines for forecasts, always with labels.
- Illustrative design data must be labelled as such.

Exploration lockups in `concepts/` use live Helvetica Neue / Arial / sans-serif
text. Final logo assets in `assets/` contain outlined type. No font files are
distributed. Final mockups, tokens and the handoff guide are in this folder.

## Reference research

The logo-design skill's local travel/transport catalogue is small (4 entries),
so it is not treated as a market survey. Framer, Pagekit and Kotlin were inspected
as examples of economical geometry and negative space, not copied. The library
was also searched for horizon and arrow concepts. No trademark clearance is
claimed.
