# Challenge rules reference

Source: FraAlliance AI Week 2026 case PDF, page 1, sections 2 and 3. This is a reference summary, not operational clearance. Source PDF supplied by Philipp on 30 October 2026.

Frankfurt has Northwest 07L/25R and South 07R/25L mainly for landings; Centre 07C/25C and West 18 for departures. The 07/25 runways switch together airport-wide; mixed directions are not possible in this case. OD25 is westbound 250 degrees true; OD07 eastbound 070 degrees true. Runway18 departures are only southbound, 180 degrees true, in either OD. It cannot be reversed for northbound takeoff.

Only the vector component from behind is tailwind. Departure tailwind must be below 15 knots, landing tailwind below 10 knots. Crosswind above 40 knots including gusts closes a runway. Landing tailwind at least10 knots forces an immediate OD switch. Smaller positive tailwind can be tolerated up to30 minutes; if it persists longer, switch to the headwind direction. Runway18 tailwind at least15 knots prevents takeoff; the case directs departures via Centre 07C/25C instead. Known missing data is not evidence of safe conditions.

Forecast rule outputs are weather suitability, not official airport closure or individual aircraft clearance. Published departure times are not wheels-off times. The project needs independent operational labels for operating-direction accuracy. Baseline rule probabilities remain null; do not present them as calibrated ML estimates.
